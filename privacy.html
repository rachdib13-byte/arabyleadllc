Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how ARABYLEAD LLC collects, uses, protects, and shares information when you visit our website, contact us, purchase digital products, or use our services.

Overview

ARABYLEAD LLC ("ARABYLEAD," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy applies to information collected through our website, contact forms, consultations, service delivery, digital product transactions, and related communications.

By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information Collected

We may collect contact details, including name, email address, business name, and message content. We may also collect business information needed to evaluate or deliver funnel, payment, automation, and marketing services.

We may collect transaction details related to purchases, invoices, service orders, or digital product access, along with website usage information such as pages viewed, referring URLs, browser type, device type, approximate location, and interaction data.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support basic website functionality, remember preferences, understand usage patterns, and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling cookies may affect some website features.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand traffic, page performance, conversions, and visitor behavior. Analytics data is used to improve our website, marketing, and service experience.

Contact Forms

When you submit a contact form, request a consultation, or email us, we use the information you provide to respond, evaluate your needs, prepare proposals, provide services, and maintain business records.

Payment Information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Stripe, PayPal, or other checkout platforms. We do not intentionally store full credit card numbers on our website. Payment providers process payment data according to their own privacy and security policies.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools for payment processing, email delivery, analytics, scheduling, customer relationship management, hosting, automation, and service delivery. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services to us or to you.

Data Protection

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain legitimate business records.

User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to certain processing of your personal information. You may also request that we stop sending marketing communications.

To exercise privacy rights, contact us using the information below. We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.
